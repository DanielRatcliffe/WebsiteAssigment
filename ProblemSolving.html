Problem Solving

Digital transformation: Brick Oven Pizza

Brick Oven Pizza is a fictional local restaurant that currently relies on telephone enquiries and printed menus. Customers have limited access to menu information online, while staff spend time answering routine questions and taking table requests by phone.

Acting as the restaurant’s Chief Technology Officer, I am developing a web prototype that brings its menu, offers and contact information together. A booking-request form will allow customers to prepare an email containing their preferred date, time and party size.

The proposed solution aims to improve customer engagement, make information easier to access and reduce the staff time spent collecting booking details.

Demonstration only: this restaurant is fictional. The prototype will not process payments or confirm real reservations.
