Digital transformation: Brick Oven Pizza

Brick Oven Pizza is a fictional local restaurant that currently relies on telephone enquiries and printed menus. Customers have limited access to menu information online, while staff spend time answering routine questions and taking table requests by phone.

Acting as the restaurant’s Chief Technology Officer, I am developing a web prototype that brings its menu, offers and contact information together. A booking-request form will allow customers to prepare an email containing their preferred date, time and party size.

The proposed solution aims to improve customer engagement, make information easier to access and reduce the staff time spent collecting booking details.

Demonstration only: this restaurant is fictional. The prototype will not process payments or confirm real reservations.

How your booking details are used

The name, email address, preferred date and time, number of guests and optional message you enter are used to prepare a booking request email. These details are processed in your browser. This website does not save booking details to a server or database, use them for marketing, or send emails automatically.

Selecting “Prepare booking email” displays a preview and attempts to open the draft in your email application. That application and its provider may save the draft according to their own settings and privacy policies. Your browser may also remember information entered into forms.

The restaurant email address is fictional. Please do not send the draft: this demonstration cannot accept real bookings.

Cookies and external services

This demonstration does not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.
